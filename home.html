ABOUT US

I'm Aleshba Ali, a passionate makeup and henna artist. My journey began in 10th grade, experimenting with eyeshadow and false lashes, which sparked my love for beauty. At the same time, I picked up a henna cone and started creating designs on mirrors, cardboard, and my own hands—turning a hobby into a craft. What started as fun soon became a profession. I began applying makeup on friends, then clients, and with the support of my family, I launched my own beauty brand. Over time, my work gained recognition, leading to PR collaborations and my journey as an influencer. Today, I provide professional makeup & henna services while sharing my passion with the world. What began as a small step has become a dream come true—and this is just the beginning. 💄✨ 🚀 Welcome to my world of beauty & creativity!
